# Precise UI geometry and composition — 2026-11-10

This owner-requested pass supersedes the smaller register, fixed-width Boost and separate list-column scrollbar in [UI layout polish](history/UI_LAYOUT_POLISH.md). It changes the actual root game, preserving the room, cabinet, title, ticket artwork, production data/actions and original worksheet/Packages interfaces.

## Current structure (Orders revision, 2026-11-10)

- The cabinet header uses a shared 48px left/right inset, an automatically sized right column and one vertical centerline. Menu Collection keeps its original aspect ratio; Boost hugs its diamond, live value, 10px gap and 24px horizontal padding. Its cream pill is drawn in CSS with concentric outlines. Only the home button uses the simple rainbow SVG diamond; existing popup artwork remains unchanged. Shimmer and reduced-motion support remain.
- Both Orders utility controls use matching 44px illustrated pills inside the raised wood face, with transparent touch targets at least 44 rendered pixels high. Shared anchors align their centers; the plaque-to-pill and pill-to-well gaps stay compact. Labels, green Skip state and original refresh/confirmation controllers remain intact.
- `.board-front` owns the title and utilities. Its native CSS wood extension connects the original plaque area to the new recessed opening without scaling the original clip/plaque. The board retains its 410×727 logical bounds at x1430/y149, cabinet-body alignment and window clearance. The opening begins after the title, 12px gap, responsive utility target and 18px lower margin.
- `.board-list` follows the raster's carved seam at y229, inset 48px on both sides and 55px above the base, with 24px upper and 18px lower corner radii. There is no opaque rectangular well or separate drawn border laid over the art. The viewport clips scrolling paper at this boundary; equal 10px horizontal list padding centers every die-cut slip. Three complete slips and part of a fourth fit at rest. The matched ticket masks and complete list remain.
- The Orders rail is a sibling of the opening, mounted on the right stile (11px from the board's right edge). The track and visible thumb share the same horizontal center. Independent rail and thumb border-image slices preserve the decorative caps; only their center fills extend. The 15px thumb artwork has a separate 44px touch target. The track remains above the arrows' expanded targets so clicks at its limits reach the track.
- `src/ui/illustrated-controls.js` now uses fractional computed track/thumb heights to calculate travel. The native scroll ratio maps to exactly `0 … trackHeight − thumbHeight`, avoiding gaps caused by rounded `clientHeight`. At maximum scroll the thumb anchors directly to `bottom:0`, eliminating the last subpixel rounding remainder. Native swipe, pointer capture, wheel, track clicks, arrows, Home/End and keyboard scrolling remain.
- Tickets show reward artwork beside the main food/quantity/multiplication/tray/quantity row, followed by consistently aligned Progress, Resume or Collect state. Star counts and the icon/order-type row are absent from visible markup; actual order type and reward names remain in accessible button labels. A fixed 60px reward column, larger food/tray images, 32px math (28px for four-digit operands), and an 18px progress label replace the smaller three-row layout. All values/actions still come from production tickets/runs.
- The art-only redraw retains these footprints, viewports and masks. Eight tiers use cream, mint, blue, purple, yellow/gold, coral/red, regal gold and rainbow treatments; independent replacement illustrations live in `assets/bakery-ui/illustrated/`. Matching inks, edges and progress fills reinforce the material; reward art and progressively richer corner trim, rays, sparkles, upper-tier glow/shine and gold accents provide non-color cues. Effects remain behind readable content and respect reduced motion.
- The register is 280px wide, a 16.7% increase from 240px, with its original aspect ratio, orientation, bell and cutout. A shared counter baseline (`bottom:226px`) keeps its doily grounded. It overlaps the lower list naturally. The list's lower content padding lets every ticket, including the last one, scroll fully above it.
